About

Evidence people can act on

I design and run the systems that tell humanitarian teams whether their work is reaching people and making a difference: indicator frameworks, mobile data collection, data quality audits, and community feedback mechanisms that people actually use.

My work has taken me from boat-based data quality visits in South Sudan's flood zones to designing Johanniter's first integrated PMEAL framework in Cabo Delgado, leading strategic information for USAID Ethiopia's health office, and now managing M&E for Samaritan's Purse in Lebanon.

I hold an MBA in Non-Profit Project Management and a BSc in Information Systems, and I've spent much of my career bringing digital tools (mobile forms, GIS, DHIS2, even drones) into program monitoring.
